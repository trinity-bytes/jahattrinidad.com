### The sky I loved went unnoticed

The first version had a sky behind the canvas and light frosted glass panels. After a few days of real use, nobody noticed the sky and the glass no longer fit. On day 11 (PR #1) I cleared the canvas to a solid dark colour, swapped the glass for solid cartoon panels and made navigation faster. Glass came back on day 35: dark, 75% opaque, and tuned so text stays at WCAG AA even over a pure white card.

### An agent wiped the tags of 58 items

On day 53 an agent cleaned up the garden for me, and 58 items lost every tag. The API read a missing tags field as an empty list: a Zod default survives .partial(), so a patch that only fixed a title also cleared the tags. I restored them from a local snapshot, and hotfix v1.2.1 shipped the same day: an omitted list now keeps the stored one.
