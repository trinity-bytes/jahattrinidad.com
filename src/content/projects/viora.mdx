### The web and API did not agree on every field

During the Vue backend integration, sign-up role names and billing plan codes needed fixes. The July 6–7 commits record those corrections; the first interface contract was not the final one. The earlier Angular surveillance merge was also reverted on May 14 and the module continued the next day. The history does not explain why it was reverted.

The team built the product together. My work included the web alerts and shared layout, parts of the Spring Boot API and assistant, Android harvest settlements, and Flutter manager alerts and plot dossiers.
