### The field test changed the interface

During Android validation, one producer struggled to locate a plot with GPS off. Another traced a plot much smaller than the area she had declared and asked for clearer explanations of agronomic terms and cold alerts outside the app.

I added a GPS-off prompt with a manual drawing option, an area mismatch confirmation, contextual explanations, a consistent daily summary and critical system notifications. WhatsApp was requested, but these changes use Android notifications; they are not a WhatsApp integration. A teammate handled the adjustable calendar and discarded olives.

The team built Viora together. My work also included web alerts and layout, parts of the API and assistant, offline harvest settlements, and manager alerts and plot dossiers in Flutter.
