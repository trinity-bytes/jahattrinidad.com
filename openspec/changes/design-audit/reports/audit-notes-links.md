## 3. Patterns

- **Sticky nav without an anchor offset.** NL-01. Any in-page link that lands on an element under the 69 px sticky nav has the same problem; one rule (`:target, h2[id] { scroll-margin-top: 6rem }` in the base styles) covers the post and any future heading anchor.
- **No single mono-label token.** NL-10 (three greens), NL-11 (four trackings), NL-14 (two cases, three orders), NL-15 (label = item style), NL-20 (lowercase tags), and HO-02 for markers. One `.label` token (Plex 12/16, 500, 0.06em, uppercase, green-700) and one meta formatter (`date · reading time`) fix all of them.
- **Read-mode type has no small-screen step.** NL-04 (h2 and body unchanged at 390), NL-07 (code 12 vs 17), NL-19 (inline weight). The post set its ramp at 1440 only; a 390 step for prose h2/body/code is one media query.
- **Actions without feedback.** NL-02 (Copy), NL-12 (invisible hover), HO-05. Define hover and pressed per control type once.
- **Strings built in components.** NL-03: a dictionary value is completed with an English word inside the component template. Keep whole phrases in the dictionaries (functions such as `readingTime(minutes)`), never fragments joined in markup.

## 5. Strengths

- **Prose rhythm is exact.** At 1440 and 768: h2 48 above / 16 below, paragraphs and the code block 28 apart, with no drift across all four sections; 390 uses the design's 40 / 12. The contents heading and the first paragraph share their top (y 680 at 1440).
- **The link-arrow rule holds on all three pages.** `→` for internal cards and "Write to me", `←` for "All notes", Lucide `arrow-up-right` with `target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener noreferrer"` and the visually hidden new-tab note on RSS, Elsewhere, the CV card and every logo button; no arrow on Copy.
- **`/links/` geometry is precise.** Card arrows sit on the card's optical centre (card 222–296.6, icon 249.3–269.3, both centred at 259.3), logo targets are 52 × 52 with 10 px gaps, cards are 74.6 px tall, and the two columns are centred on each other (both centres at y 439.5).
- **The code block is keyboard-reachable** (`pre` has `tabindex="0"`), and its Shiki colours follow the approved brand theme.
