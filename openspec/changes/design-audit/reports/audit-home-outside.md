## 3. Patterns

- **No link component (HO-01, HO-13, HO-14, HO-15, HO-19).** Forward links are assembled four ways (span + glyph, anchor + glyph flex, SVG inside a pill, footer `↗` SVG at another size). One `TextLink` with `kind="forward|back|external"` that owns the underline, the arrow drawing, its size (em-relative) and the 44 px hit area fixes all five at once.
- **No section-header component (HO-02, HO-17, HO-16).** Each section writes its own marker, h2 and padding. A `SectionHeader` (marker + h2 with fixed gap, tracking, line height) and one section padding token per breakpoint remove the drift.
- **State tokens missing (HO-05, HO-06, HO-18, HO-19).** Hover, focus and press are defined per file. Three tokens — `--focus-ring` (3 px, offset 3, colour per surface), a hover rule per control type, `--press-scale` — close all four.
- **Icon stroke tied to size (HO-12, HO-13).** Fixed `stroke-width=2` in `Icon.astro` makes every size its own weight.
- **Touch targets decided per section (HO-07, HO-08).** The 44 px rule was applied to section links but not to the footer and tablet nav.

## 5. Strengths

- **Icon optical centring is exact.** Every icon beside text is within 0–0.9 px of the label's cap-height centre at all three widths (hero arrow +0.9, contact icons +0.2, footer `↗` 0.0), and icon–label gaps are consistent within each group (8 / 4 px).
- **The mobile menu's behaviour matches its spec**: focus moves to "Work" on open, Tab cycles inside the sheet (links → languages → profiles → logo → Contact → Close), Esc closes and returns focus to "Open menu", `aria-expanded` and the label flip, the lens dock is removed, page scroll is locked, every segment and profile button is a full 44 px target, profile buttons have network names plus the new-tab note.
- **Reduced motion is handled at the source**: smooth scroll stops, the magnetic CTA and parallax are skipped, the nav pill drops to a 120 ms fade, and the menu animates only without `reduce`.
- **The link-arrow rule holds** in this area: every external link has `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"` (profiles also `me`), the `↗` icon and the `.visually-hidden` new-tab note; in-page actions (Copy my email, chips, Send, the shortcut toggle) carry no arrow; text links keep a 44 px hit area through `min-height`.
